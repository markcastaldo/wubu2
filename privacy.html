~/privacy.txt

short version: the only personal data this site collects is your email address, and only if you sign up for the newsletter. it's used for one thing: telling you when new free packs and releases drop. no ads, no analytics, no tracking, never sold or shared.

who we are

wubu2, an electronic music project based in the United Kingdom. we're the data controller for the newsletter. contact: wubu2music@duck.com

the newsletter

what
your email address, when you signed up, when you confirmed, and the wording you agreed to (so we can show you gave consent)
why
to email you about new wubu2 free packs and releases. nothing else
legal basis
your consent (UK GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). you sign up, then confirm by clicking a link in an email. nobody is added without that click
where
a private Google Sheet in wubu2's Google account. emails are sent with Gmail. Google may store data outside the UK (e.g. the US) under its own data protection safeguards
how long
until you unsubscribe. then your address is deleted from the list. sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 30 days
tracking
none. no open tracking, no click tracking, no pixels

to leave: click unsubscribe at the bottom of any email, or use the unsubscribe button your mail app shows next to the sender. one click, done.

your rights

you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to fix or delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time. email wubu2music@duck.com and we'll sort it within a month. if you're not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk

the website

no cookies of our own, no analytics. your browser keeps one temporary note (in session storage, never sent anywhere) so the intro animation only plays once per visit. the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses for security. fonts load from Google Fonts. the Spotify player on the home page comes from Spotify and follows Spotify's privacy policy. free pack downloads come from GitHub.
