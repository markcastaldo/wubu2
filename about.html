Striped McCoy is an electronic avant-pop project from rural Shropshire, England. Synths, lo-fi guitar and leftover scraps of digital recordings get stitched together into fizzy, slightly stressed-out pop songs.

The tracks pair melancholy lyrics with bouncy, bright instrumentals, pulling from bedroom pop, indie, IDM and industrial dance along the way. Releases go back to 2020 (It's All a Mess, Really., I Think, Therefore I Am), with a run of singles through 2025 including Left In The Dark, Never On Time and The Sickening Machine.
